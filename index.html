What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product-organizing list used to save links, group categories, and compare details while researching purchases. An entry alone does not verify quality, authenticity, availability, or seller reliability. This independent guide offers category browsing and a research workflow; it is not an official Oopbuy service or a downloadable spreadsheet.

Oopbuy spreadsheet guide

Less scrolling.
More useful comparisons.

Treat an Oopbuy product spreadsheet as a research notebook. Keep each row specific enough that you can explain why one listing belongs on your shortlist.

Browse product categories ↗

  1. Choose a category and a purpose

    Separate everyday T-shirts from layering pieces, sports jerseys, and accessories. Add the fit, color, and use you are looking for.

  2. Capture the listing details

    Save the product URL, selected variant, listed price, measurements, material description, and the date you checked them.

  3. Compare costs and evidence

    Separate item price from delivery and other charges. Review relevant photos and seller terms; ask about anything the listing leaves unclear.

  4. Recheck before you commit

    Confirm the selected size, current stock, final charges, and applicable return conditions. Old spreadsheet notes may no longer match a live listing.

What should I check before choosing a clothing size?

Compare the listing's garment measurements with a similar item you own. Check the measurement unit, fit notes, fabric description, and any stated tolerance rather than relying on the size label alone.

Does a listed price include shipping and import charges?

Do not assume it does. Check whether the price includes domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, taxes, and any applicable import charges. Verify the current total before paying.

Do brand reference images prove that a CAFANS listing is authentic?

No. The brand images on this page are visual references. They do not verify the authenticity, stock, quality, or availability of any CAFANS listing, and they do not imply brand endorsement.

How can I keep an Oopbuy product spreadsheet useful?

Record when you checked each listing, remove unavailable entries, and update changed prices or variants. Keep unanswered questions visible and group items by category so comparisons remain relevant.
